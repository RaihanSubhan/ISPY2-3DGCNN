## Important scientific limitation

These visuals are conceptual and thesis-facing. They do not measure true blood flow, milk flow, or fluid flow. The correct scientific wording is contrast-transport or enhancement disturbance proxy.

## How to use this in the thesis

Use these figures to explain the proposed biological intuition and future graph-learning idea. The main validated pilot result remains the official MRI/FTV support-data pCR prediction model.
