# Stage 14B Cohort 3D Tumor-Model Gallery

Rendered real-mask-derived 3D tumor models for 6 cohort patients in one figure.
Each panel shows a marching-cubes tumor surface plus its spatial tumor-node graph
(blue = core, orange = periphery). Edges are spatial links, not vessels or measured flow.

- ISPY2-109623 (voxels=284829)
- ISPY2-119928 (voxels=9054)

- ISPY2-640927 (voxels=298674)
